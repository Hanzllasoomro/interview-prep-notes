# Global Interview Preparation Notes

A community-driven open-source repository where people from around the world can share interview preparation notes for different job roles, skills, companies, and experience levels.

## Goal

The goal of this repository is to make interview preparation accessible to everyone, especially students, fresh graduates, career switchers, and job seekers who cannot afford expensive courses.

## How to Use This Repo

Browse notes by:

- Job role
- Skill
- Experience level
- Company type
- Interview round

## How to Contribute

You can contribute by:

- Adding interview notes
- Improving existing notes
- Adding questions and answers
- Sharing real interview experiences
- Adding resources
- Fixing mistakes
- Translating notes into other languages

## Contribution Rules

Please make sure your contribution is:

- Clear and useful
- Original or properly credited
- Free from private company confidential information
- Well-structured
- Respectful and inclusive

## Folder Structure

- `jobs/` contains role-based preparation notes
- `skills/` contains topic-based notes
- `companies/` contains company-type preparation
- `templates/` contains contribution templates

## License

This project is open-source and available for everyone.

## Why Contribute?

Every note you add can help someone prepare better, feel more confident, and possibly get their dream job.

Many people around the world do not have access to paid courses, mentors, or expensive interview preparation resources. By contributing to this repository, you are helping create a free and open knowledge base for everyone.

Your small contribution can become someone else’s big opportunity.
